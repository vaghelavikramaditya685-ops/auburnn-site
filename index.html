Measuring tips

  • Wear the undergarments you'll wear on the day.
  • Keep the tape snug, not tight, and level with the floor.
  • For lengths, wear the heels you'll wear to the event.

The fine print, kept simple

Returns, refunds & policies

Draft wording based on common practice for Indian couture labels. Auburnn to confirm before launch.

Custom (bespoke) orders

Because each piece is cut and stitched to your measurements, custom orders cannot be returned or exchanged. If something doesn't fit because of our stitching, we'll alter it free of charge within 15 days of delivery. If the measurements provided were incorrect, alterations are available at a nominal charge. Overseas clients pay return shipping for alterations.

Ready-to-wear returns & exchanges

Unworn pieces with tags intact can be exchanged or returned for store credit within 7 days of delivery. Pieces altered at your request, sale items and dupattas sold alone are final sale. Message us on WhatsApp to start an exchange; we'll share the return address.

Cancellations

Custom orders can be cancelled for a full refund within 48 hours of paying the advance. After fabric has been bought or cut, the advance is non-refundable. Ready-to-wear orders can be cancelled any time before dispatch.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed within 7 to 10 working days to the original payment method. International refunds may take longer depending on your bank. Shipping charges are non-refundable.

Damaged or wrong item

Please record an unboxing video and tell us within 48 hours of delivery. We'll repair, replace or refund at no cost to you.

Colour & handwork

Every piece is handcrafted, so slight variations in colour, embroidery and mirror placement are part of its character, not defects. Screen colours may differ slightly from the fabric.

Care

Dry clean only. Store in the muslin bag provided, away from damp and direct sunlight. Keep heavy pieces folded rather than hung.

Your measurements & privacy

We use your details and measurements only to make and ship your outfit, and keep them on file for future orders. We never share them. Ask us anytime to delete them.
